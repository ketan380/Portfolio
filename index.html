From technical diploma foundations to engineering degree specializations in backend architectures.

I am an Information Technology engineering student who started with a 3-year Diploma in Computer Engineering at KCE Society's College of Engineering & Management, driven by how software works beneath the surface.

My diploma education grounded me in the fundamentals of computing—structured programming, memory management, operating system layers, and relational schema theory.
